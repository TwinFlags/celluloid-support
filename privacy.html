Privacy Policy

This policy covers the App Store release of Celluloid for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Writing and storage

Celluloid saves your Project Books and drafts on your device. The App Store version does not require a Celluloid account or send your writing to a Celluloid server. Celluloid does not use advertising, tracking, or developer analytics.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on Sync with iCloud, Celluloid uses your private Apple iCloud database to sync writing between your devices. Apple handles iCloud under its Privacy Policy. You can stop syncing by turning off Sync with iCloud in Celluloid Settings and reopening the app.

Exports and deletion

You choose where exported files go and who receives them. You can delete drafts and Project Books from their menus. If iCloud sync is enabled, deletions sync to your other devices. Manage exported copies wherever you saved them.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, your email address and the details you choose to share are used to respond to your request. To ask a privacy question or request deletion of a support email, contact enderasthra@gmail.com.
