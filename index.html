Celluloid Support

Need help with Celluloid? Email enderasthra@gmail.com.

Include your device, system version, Celluloid version, and what you were trying to do.

Back up or restore a Project Book

In the Library, open the Project Book’s menu and choose Export Backup. To open a backup, choose Restore from the Library menu.

Export a screenplay

Open the screenplay’s menu and choose Export. Celluloid can export PDF, Final Draft, and Fountain files.

Sync across devices

Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device, enable Sync with iCloud in Celluloid Settings, then close and reopen Celluloid.
